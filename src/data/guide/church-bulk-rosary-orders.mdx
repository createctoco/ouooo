<GuideSection eyebrow="Sizing" title="Choose the rosary around the person receiving it">
  <p>
    A rosary always has the same number of beads, so bead size controls weight, volume and how comfortably it can be
    held. For children receiving a First Communion rosary, a smaller, lighter bead is easier to hold through a service
    and less likely to be dropped or tangled. For Confirmation candidates and adult congregations, an adult-size rosary
    is the safer default.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    If a single order covers several age groups, consider one consistent style in two sizes rather than several
    unrelated designs. It keeps the programme visually coherent, simplifies reordering, and makes it obvious which
    rosary belongs to which group when they are distributed.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection
  eyebrow="Stock or custom"
  title="Decide early which part of the order needs production"
  intro="The two paths have very different timing, so the decision should be made before the date is fixed."
  background="cream"
>
  <p>
    In-stock catalogue items are the fastest route: most styles are held in quantity, can be mixed in one shipment, and
    are typically dispatched within about ten days of order confirmation. If the programme needs a specific colour, a
    parish name, a custom card or a different pendant, that is a production decision rather than a stock decision, and
    it needs a sample and a written specification before bulk work begins.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    A practical split is to use stock items for the main distribution and a small custom element, such as an insert card
    or a printed pouch, for the part that carries the parish identity. That keeps the quantity that must be produced
    under control while still making the order feel specific to the community.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection eyebrow="After delivery" title="Keep a reference for the next order">
  <p>
    Keep the approved sample, the written specification and a photo of the packed item. When the next First Communion or
    Christmas season arrives, repeating a known product is faster and less risky than starting from a new design. A
    repeatable core range, replenished before each peak, is usually the most reliable way for a parish or school to buy
    rosaries.
