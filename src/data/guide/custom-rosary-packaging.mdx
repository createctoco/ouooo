<GuideSection eyebrow="Customization" title="What can actually be private-labelled">
  <p>
    On a rosary programme, the packaging is usually the easiest part to brand. Typical options include a printed logo on
    a pouch or box, a branded insert card, a hangtag, a barcode label, and private-label information that replaces the
    supplier name. The product itself can also be customized through bead material, colour, size, pendant style and
    centerpiece, but those changes affect tooling and minimum quantities far more than a printed card does.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    A practical sequence is to start with an insert card or hangtag on an existing product, confirm that the branding
    reads correctly in the target language, and only then move to a printed box. This keeps the first order small and
    gives you a physical sample to judge print quality, colour and logo placement.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection
  eyebrow="Cost and minimums"
  title="Why packaging changes the minimum order quantity"
  intro="Packaging cost per unit is only half the picture. The other half is the setup behind it."
  background="cream"
>
  <p>
    A stock OPP bag or a plain pouch can usually be added to a normal production or stock order. A printed box is a
    different kind of purchase: it needs artwork, a dieline, a printing run and often a minimum quantity per design.
    That minimum applies to the packaging, not necessarily to the rosary, so buyers sometimes discover that the box
    costs more to set up than the product inside it.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    Ask for the packaging minimum separately from the product minimum, and confirm whether the quoted price includes
    printing, barcode labelling and insertion labour. If the answer is a range, ask what changes it: quantity, number of
    colours, material, or the number of SKUs sharing one design.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection eyebrow="Next step" title="Confirm the pack before you confirm the order">
  <p>
    Packaging is one of the few parts of a wholesale order that can be sampled cheaply and judged physically. Request a
    pre-production sample with the actual artwork, check it against your listing photos, and keep the approved sample
    and written specification as the reference for bulk production. If a detail is not written down, it is not agreed.
  </p>
