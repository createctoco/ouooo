    title="A cloth scapular and a scapular pendant are different products."
  >
    A scapular in the traditional sense is a devotional garment: two small panels of cloth joined by straps and worn
    over the shoulders. A scapular pendant is a piece of devotional jewellery shaped like a scapular. Both are
    legitimate products, but they are not interchangeable, and describing one as the other is one of the most common
    errors in religious goods catalogues.
  </GuideCallout>

<GuideSection eyebrow="Devotional care" title="Describe the item accurately and without exaggeration">
  <p>
    A scapular is a sacramental: a devotional object used to support prayer and a Christian way of life. It is not a
    charm and should not be marketed as a guarantee of protection, success or salvation. That distinction matters for
    both religious respect and product compliance, because exaggerated claims can mislead buyers and damage trust.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    The same applies to the investiture of the Brown Scapular, which is normally carried out by a priest. A supplier can
    describe the object accurately and note that enrolment is arranged locally; it should not imply that the item
    arrives already invested or blessed.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection
  eyebrow="Customization"
  title="What can be personalised, and what needs a sample"
  intro="Scapulars are often ordered for a specific parish, school or ministry, so customisation is common."
  background="cream"
>
  <p>
    Typical custom work includes a parish or school name, a devotional image, thread colours, a printed prayer card and
    individual packaging. Printed images and embroidered text should always be approved on a physical sample before bulk
    production, because colour reproduction and stitch density are difficult to judge from a digital proof.
  </p>
  <p class="mt-5">
    If the scapular is intended as a gift, confirm whether the packaging is a simple bag, a pouch, or a printed card.
    For parish distribution, individual bagging is usually the most practical option; for retail, a pouch or a branded
    card presents better.
  </p>
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection eyebrow="Next step" title="Approve the sample, then the bulk order">
  <p>
    Scapulars are small, inexpensive items, which makes the cost of a sample low and the value of approving one high.
    Check the panel size, strap length, image placement and colour against the written specification, and keep the
    approved sample as the reference for production. If a detail is not written down, it is not agreed.
